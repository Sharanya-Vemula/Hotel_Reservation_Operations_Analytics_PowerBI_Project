# 🏨 Hotel Booking and Stay Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Hotel Booking and Stay Performance Analytics Dashboard** is a Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze hotel booking trends, revenue performance, guest behaviour, stay patterns, staff performance, and operational challenges.

The main objective of this project is to transform hotel booking data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), helping hotel management make data-driven decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall hotel booking and revenue performance.
- Identify booking trends across hotels and booking channels.
- Understand guest behaviour and loyalty categories.
- Monitor stay performance and completion rates.
- Evaluate staff performance and room utilization.
- Analyze booking cancellations and guest no-shows.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI development.
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing and managing business data.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

The project consists of six interactive dashboard pages:

### 1. Executive Summary
Provides an overview of hotel business performance through total bookings, booking revenue, total stays, completion rate, cancellation rate, and no-show rate.

### 2. Booking Demand Analysis
Analyzes hotel-wise bookings, booking channels, room type preferences, monthly booking trends, and revenue contribution.

### 3. Guest Behaviour Analysis
Explores guest categories, loyalty tiers, guest locations, and customer booking behaviour.

### 4. Stay Performance Analysis
Monitors total stays, completed stays, average stay duration, hotel-wise performance, and service requests.

### 5. Staff and Room Performance
Evaluates staff activity, department-wise performance, staff ratings, room categories, and room utilization.

### 6. Booking and Stay Problems
Analyzes cancellations, no-show rates, service requests, and potential operational issues across hotels.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify important business patterns:

- Understand booking demand and revenue contribution across hotels.
- Identify popular booking channels and room preferences.
- Analyze customer segments and loyalty categories.
- Monitor stay completion and service request patterns.
- Evaluate staff activity and room utilization.
- Identify hotels with comparatively higher cancellation and no-show rates.
